# 0001 — EVT/MVP requirements

Status: Baseline for task 0001  
Date: 2026-08-30

These are testable targets, not claims about an unfinished device. “EVT” means the engineering-validation prototype; “product candidate” means the later integrated build.

## Product and interaction

| ID | Requirement | Verification |
|---|---|---|
| PR-01 | The product candidate fits within 95 × 60 × 30 mm. The stretch target is 80 × 56 × 26 mm. | Calipers and mass/volume record |
| PR-02 | The face and conversation-button indicator visibly distinguish private idle from a live session and compose user-speech, assistant-speech, thinking, offline, and low-battery signals. User and assistant speech indications can overlap. | Parallel-state transition test and video |
| PR-03 | A physical action changes the face within 100 ms; animation sustains 30 fps without audio dropouts. | Instrumented UI test |
| PR-04 | The product exposes exactly two physical controls: the illuminated conversation button and a latching power slide switch ([ADR 0008](../decisions/0008_use_exactly_two_physical_controls.md)). On the custom carrier, one capture-enable command net is hardware-biased inactive through reset/boot/crash/watchdog/recovery/OTA, gates the microphone path, and drives the cyan indicator, so firmware cannot command those states independently; component/open/short/stuck faults are still tested and only hard power-off is hardware-certain. An ordinary claimed boot without the recovery chord returns to private idle and never resumes capture without a fresh conversation-button press; factory-first-boot and recovery are capture-gated setup exceptions. Volume is touchscreen/app-controlled, not a physical control. | Power-off continuity/backfeed and switch-bounce tests with battery/USB/debug/modem attached; capture-enable/indicator component/command/reset fault injection; and normal/setup/recovery no-auto-capture tests |
| PR-05 | One conversation-button press requests a session, amber indicates connection with uplink still gated, and cyan indicates that listening is actually live. A second press locally closes capture and playback from any session state without waiting for the network. While cyan `LIVE`, EVT supports continuous concurrent capture/playback, automatic endpointing, and speech-driven barge-in without per-utterance presses. The shipping button never changes to a hold-to-talk gesture; inability to sustain safe full duplex closes the session and reports an unavailable/degraded state. | Scripted live session, connection-fault, invariant-button, and forced-resource-failure test |
| PR-06 | No camera capture is enabled in MVP. The CoreS3 EVT camera has an opaque cover and no initialized driver/clock; production omits the sensor. | Physical inspection, firmware configuration, and traffic inspection |
| PR-07 | **Mandatory on-device MVP gate:** the pager screen displays large, high-contrast sliding caption text directly below the eyes while rendering the assistant's speech during playback; each new caption enters right-to-left from beyond the visible edge, and the empty caption position has no placeholder, border, or colored panel. Gateway-forwarded additions are keyed to the active `response_id`/`output_id`, queued without resetting the active track, and presented from the device's rendered-sample cursor. On-device motion proceeds smoothly from the current rendered position instead of snapping word by word or jumping backward within an active response. The caption never runs ahead of rendered audio by more than one caption segment, first caption text appears within 500 ms of first rendered audio, and on barge-in or truncation the displayed caption is trimmed to the words actually heard. User-speech captions are a configuration option, not an MVP gate; history opt-in may still request a machine input transcript without displaying it. The caption is legible at arm's length on the 2-inch EVT screen. An app-only caption does not satisfy this requirement. | Instrumented on-device caption/audio timing and queue trace, idle/live visual-state inspection, right-edge entry and slow-motion continuity/backward-jump review, barge-in trim test, history-with-captions-hidden test, and 2-inch-screen legibility check |
| PR-08 | The two pupil-free ivory eyes use deterministic local expression arbitration independent of the capture/session reducer. Activity owns truthful conversation movement; battery capacity owns energy/openness and is not hidden by charging; expression geometry selects fault/low capacity before an allowlisted expiring model tool hint before an activity default before mood; active conversation gaze outranks idle curiosity. A low-amplitude continuous layer changes rhythm with emotional state, while large gaze remains brief conjugate saccades separated by stable fixations. The resting face initially has a calm, centered gaze. During eligible idle at normal capacity, the first randomized gesture begins after 3–5 seconds and later gestures begin after centered pauses of 6–12 seconds. Each 1.6–2.4 second gesture moves both complete eyes together in a look-up, look-down, side-to-side look-around, or clockwise/counterclockwise scan and always returns them to center. Shape, height, width, tilt, spacing, and controlled asymmetry distinguish at least 25 allowlisted emotions. The Realtime model selects emotion through a constrained cosmetic function call before speech; control metadata is neither spoken nor captioned. Speech, thinking, connection/fault state, low battery, screen hiding, or reduced-motion preference cancels/suppresses large random gaze immediately; reduced motion also disables the continuous cosmetic layer. No expression can alter or imply the amber/cyan/off capture indicator, and no mouth/lips are rendered. Cosmetic animation is optional and cannot be a dependency of conversation Start/Stop, capture truth, or the session reducer; import or initialization failure leaves a static centered fallback, and a later timer/animation fault cannot remove the already-wired control path. | Pure channel-priority/timer/tool-contract tests with injected randomness, first/repeat-delay and center-return tests, broad emotion allowlist and metadata-not-captioned inspection, browser-timer-receiver regression test, low-battery-while-charging/reduced-motion/visibility cancellation tests, optional-load/control-order inspection, state-indicator separation inspection, and video |

V1-A's browser caption is a deliberately slower visual reference, not PR-07 timing evidence: every assistant response starts beyond the right edge and moves left at a fixed 60 CSS px/s independently of audio/token cadence and may trail it; transcript additions queue ahead without retargeting the motion; the track has no easing or per-word velocity changes; interruption freezes the track; reset snaps to zero; and `prefers-reduced-motion` snaps position updates. CoreS3 and product firmware must still satisfy the rendered-audio cursor, lead, latency, and heard-boundary requirements above.

## Voice quality and service

| ID | Requirement | Verification |
|---|---|---|
| VO-01 | Speech is intelligible at arm's length in a quiet room without holding the device against the mouth. | Recorded word-error and listener sample |
| VO-02a | On stable Wi-Fi, median acoustic user-speech-end to first audible response is at most 1,200 ms and p95 is at most 2,000 ms, including endpoint detection. | At least 50 timestamped speech segments |
| VO-02b | Local speech end, provider `speech_stopped`, response creation, provider first audio, device receipt, and first rendered sample are timestamped separately. | Correlated trace review |
| VO-02c | Across at least 100 intended utterances containing 200–800 ms internal pauses, fillers, short corrections, and quiet/moderate noise, there are at most two early cutoffs and at most five missed endpoints, where a miss means no response creation within 2 seconds of the labelled speech end. Silence/nonspeech noise causes at most one spurious response per 30 minutes. Non-addressed background-speech responses are measured separately, and never authorize a consequential tool without owner confirmation. | Labelled endpoint corpus, event trace, and background-speech report |
| VO-03a | During assistant playback, near-end speech stops audible assistant output within 250 ms at p95 and no audio from that response resumes. | At least 100 scripted barge-ins with acoustic/event timestamps |
| VO-03b | Pressing the conversation button to close the session stops capture and playback within 250 ms, including during network or provider failure. | Automated tone, uplink-byte, and control timestamps |
| VO-04 | A 20-minute live session containing at least ten exchanges, correction, double-talk, spontaneous barge-in, and brief acknowledgements that intentionally interrupt under the MVP policy finishes without reset, stuck state, stale output, or credential exposure. | Acceptance script and logs |
| VO-05 | The model, voice, prompt, and retention settings are server configuration, not device constants. | Configuration-change test |
| VO-06 | During defined far-end-only playback, median echo-return-loss enhancement is at least 20 dB at the maximum permitted duplex volume and residual echo does not trigger local VAD. During double-talk, near-end phrase recognition degrades by no more than 10 percentage points versus a speaker-silent control at the same user positions; capture does not clip. | Paired raw/reference/clean acoustic corpus and transcription/listener score |
| VO-07 | Assistant-only playback causes at most one false cancellation per 30 assistant-speaking minutes across defined volumes, hand/table positions, and quiet/moderate-noise scenes. | At least 60 assistant-speaking minutes in the instrumented acoustic suite |
| VO-08a | The device records the rendered-sample count and rate for the active output. Against an acoustic numbered-tone ground truth, the reported heard boundary is accurate within one render frame plus the calibrated fixed hardware-output latency; this is boundary accuracy, not a network acknowledgement deadline. | Numbered-tone render-cursor test |
| VO-08b | The gateway maps the stable output ID to provider `item_id` and `content_index`, converts the rendered cursor to `audio_end_ms`, discards late chunks, and sends idempotent cancel/truncate operations. Provider acknowledgement/error latency is logged separately; timeout cannot resurrect or commit the unheard suffix. | Provider-adapter trace and conversation-state audit |
| VO-09 | Under the worst permitted simultaneous AEC, capture, playback, encrypted network, and 30 fps UI load, there are no missed audio-processing deadlines and at least 20% measured CPU headroom. A safe internal/DMA-memory low-water floor is set from mule evidence and no allocation crosses it. | Instrumented 65-minute concurrent-load soak |
| VO-10 | A 65-minute live-button intent crosses the provider's current 60-minute session limit through an early authenticated renewal: uplink is gated during handoff, raw speech is not buffered, only committed context transfers, and failure returns to private idle. | Forced and natural renewal traces |
| VO-11 | Realtime system instructions come from a versioned, server-owned template beginning `You are a companion`. The renderer accepts only fixed scalar placeholders and allowlisted user/history/retrieval/device fields, applies independent record/size limits plus a final prompt limit, serializes dynamic data as non-recursively substituted escaped JSON, rejects unknown syntax/schema and recognizable credential shapes, sanitizes retrieval URLs, and cannot be overridden through browser/device SDP. An authenticated production selector/redactor separately enforces finalization, authorization, and comprehensive MP-06 secret exclusion. Model instructions never authorize tools, capture, storage, or device-state claims. | Template snapshot, malformed/oversize/injection/credential/URL tests, browser-override test, gateway multipart inspection, context-selector audit, and tool-policy audit |

Latency and acoustic targets are ambitious gates. A half-duplex-only result fails Gate A; it triggers a breakdown of endpoint detection, local AEC/VAD, interruption propagation, uplink, model, downlink, buffering, and playback time.

## Connectivity

| ID | Requirement | Verification |
|---|---|---|
| NW-01 | Wi-Fi is the preferred path; the EVT works through a normal AP and a phone hotspot. | Two-network field test |
| NW-02a | With an already registered/warm cellular path, the system detects preferred-route loss, changes to amber/reconnecting, closes microphone uplink, discards raw/uncommitted input and queued output without replay, and restores an authenticated transport within 15 seconds. Live capture may resume only if the authenticated provider session is ready before the universal reconnect deadline in NW-02c. | Controlled short/long AP shutdown, packet inspection, and session trace |
| NW-02b | Cold modem attach from its powered-down state has a separately measured target; it is not hidden inside the 15-second warm-failover requirement. | Cold-start trials at multiple signal levels |
| NW-02c | Any recoverable route, gateway, or provider transport/session failure that enters `reconnecting` starts one non-extendable 10-second timer. The device remains amber, gates microphone uplink, and discards raw/uncommitted input and queued output without replay. It may restore cyan `LIVE` only after an authenticated provider session is ready before the deadline. At expiry, live intent clears to off/private; later recovery cannot resume capture without a fresh press, and retries or a change in failure cause cannot reset the timer. | Short/long AP, gateway, and provider outage injection; expiry timer; packet inspection; session trace |
| NW-03 | Device-to-gateway traffic uses authenticated TLS and a revocable per-device identity. | Packet inspection and credential revocation |
| NW-04 | No standard OpenAI API key is present in device flash, firmware images, logs, or network traces. | Binary/log scan and gateway audit |
| NW-05 | BLE is limited in MVP to foreground provisioning/recovery, network tests, and bounded diagnostics. Accessory pairing, Bluetooth Classic/A2DP, conversation audio, and history transport are out of scope. | ESP-IDF unified provisioning over BLE (`network_provisioning`, protocomm Security 2) including custom cell-config, claim, info, and network-test endpoints; see AP-01/AP-02/AP-05 |
| NW-06 | Cellular data volume, signal quality, reconnect count, and latency are measured during a 30–60 minute voice session. | Metered SIM and gateway logs |

## Companion app and provisioning ([ADR 0007](../decisions/0007_use_companion_app_and_cloud_history_sync.md))

| ID | Requirement | Verification |
|---|---|---|
| AP-01 | The foreground app provisions Wi-Fi credentials and the cellular profile over BLE using ESP-IDF `network_provisioning` with protocomm Security 2 and a unique per-device salt/verifier. Firmware compiles out or rejects Security 0/1, admits one commissioner, rate-limits failed proofs, and expires setup. No credential appears in plaintext on the air, in app logs/cache/analytics, or in Mochi's cloud. | iOS/Android onboarding, downgrade/proof-limit tests, BLE sniffer trace, app storage/log scan, and gateway audit |
| AP-02 | Unclaimed first boot automatically enters capture-gated setup. A claimed unit enters setup only while private-idle through its touchscreen, an online authenticated app→gateway request, or the boot-only chord (hold conversation while sliding power on for eight seconds). Factory-package proof is single-use bootstrap for initial unclaimed setup and is permanently disabled after first claim. Claim tokens are one-use and bound to account, immutable device identity, `setup_epoch`, expected `binding_generation`, and current `claim_nonce`; claim/release/recovery/revoke rotate the nonce and invalidate outstanding tokens atomically. The chord plus the current binding's recovery secret repairs networks but cannot transfer an owned binding. Transfer requires owner release or server-authorized account recovery with account verification plus a factory-key-signed, expiring physical-proof challenge bound to device/requested account/`setup_epoch`/`recovery_epoch`/expected generation/current nonce; the transfer compare-and-swaps those values, bounds/audits attempts, applies prior-owner notice/cooldown policy, revokes old credentials, and advances `binding_generation`/`claim_nonce`/`recovery_epoch` monotonically. Every claim/new binding installs a fresh recovery verifier, delivers the new recovery secret once over Security 2 for owner save/export, and rejects factory/previous-owner proofs. On release, recovery, re-claim, or account deletion, the pager purges prior-binding live/cache state plus locally stored Wi-Fi and custom cellular/APN/authentication credentials before another account can use it, while preserving immutable factory identity only. Resale release also requires confirmation that a physical SIM was removed or carrier-deactivated; lost-device revocation/account deletion direct the owner to carrier deactivation because cloud unbinding cannot cancel that plan. The numeric policy is frozen before Gate C. | First-boot/bootstrap-disable, claim-race/replay/steal, recovery-secret rotation/old-proof rejection, release/support-recovery, generation/nonce/epoch revocation, local network/cellular/state purge, physical-SIM removal/deactivation, notification/cooldown, and no-history-leak tests |
| AP-03 | The nearby cellular editor supports a signed public carrier preset or validated manual APN, PDP type, optional PAP/CHAP username/password, roaming, and enable state; it also shows read-only registration/signal status. Public preset metadata may ship/download, but custom APN/derived-profile/authentication values remain memory-only in the app and BLE-local to the pager. Physical SIM with PIN disabled is the EVT baseline, because a locked SIM needs its PIN again after cold boot. Carrier activation, billing, and subscription cancellation stay with the carrier: resale release requires physical-SIM removal/deactivation confirmation, while loss/account deletion direct carrier deactivation. Arbitrary AT commands and consumer SGP.22 eSIM are out of scope. Future SGP.32 requires a compatible modem/eUICC/eIM and carrier integration plus an explicit profile deactivation/transfer flow. Starlink Direct to Cell, if offered by a participating carrier for the selected certified modem/SIM/plan, appears only as that carrier's normal preset—not a Starlink toggle. | Preset-signature/payload audit, real-modem registration/PDP/DNS/TLS tests, cold-boot PIN check, release/loss/account-delete SIM handling, field validation, and UI inventory |
| AP-04 | Conversation-history retention is a separate explicit opt-in, default off. With retention off, no durable transcript is written to Mochi-controlled storage. With it on, the gateway stores finalized machine-transcribed user turns, the assistant text known to have been heard (or an interruption marker when the boundary is uncertain), and sanitized visible tool summaries—never raw audio, deltas, arbitrary tool payloads, or secrets. A second default-off consent controls whether retained history may be selected for future prompt context; structured memory remains a third separate consent. Item/session/all deletion removes content and propagates content-free tombstones plus derived context/index/cache invalidation; disabling future retention separately asks whether to delete existing history, and retained history remains accessible while future saving is off. Derived memory carries source IDs; source deletion defaults to cascading it, with explicit confirmation required to keep a fact. | Retention-off/retained-history/context-reuse UI and storage audit, complete-turn/interruption/tool-redaction audit, prompt-selection authorization test, item/session/all deletion propagation, derived-cache purge, and memory cascade/keep test |
| AP-05 | The app↔pager BLE session carries foreground provisioning/recovery, network tests, and bounded diagnostics only — never conversation audio or transcript content. Accessory BLE is deferred. History reaches the app exclusively through the authenticated gateway HTTPS API. | BLE lifecycle/resource and traffic audit |
| AP-06 | Provisioning runs only in capture-gated setup, with capture-enable hardware-biased inactive before firmware config, never while connecting/live/reconnecting. It remains alive through association, DNS/TLS verification, claim redemption, and atomic credential commit, then shuts BLE down and reclaims its measured provisioning memory (vendor guidance estimates roughly 110 KB) before private idle or live audio. | Reset/boot/setup state-machine, failed-network/claim recovery, plaintext-buffer, packet, and memory instrumentation tests |
| AP-07 | The gateway is authoritative for non-secret account settings. Each document is scoped to `(account_id, device_id, binding_generation)`; it assigns monotonic `config_revision` values, and both app and pager writes carry `client_mutation_id`, the current generation, and `If-Match`. A new binding starts with history retention, retained-history context, and structured-memory consent off and rejects stale-generation writes. Offline touchscreen volume may apply locally as pending; gateway conflict resolution wins with visible notice. Consent never changes before server acknowledgement. Custom Wi-Fi/APN values and capture start/stop never enter this channel. | App/pager duplicate/reordered/conflicting/offline/stale-generation mutation test, re-claim consent-reset test, and custom Wi-Fi/APN payload audit |
| AP-08 | The gateway is the sole history writer and assigns account-scoped `server_seq`; clients synchronize through an opaque cursor and immutable event IDs. Canonical data and its outbox/change row commit transactionally. Deletions use `client_mutation_id` plus expected version. Tombstones are retained through `oldest_available_seq`, and the server rejects any cursor older than that boundary so it must full-reconcile. Two app installations and a reinstallation converge after duplicate, reordered, offline, reconnect, deletion, logout, and expired-cursor scenarios. App caches are encrypted/account-scoped; their keys are device-bound/non-synchronizing, cache and key are excluded from OS backup, and a restored cache is never displayed before authentication and full reconciliation. Offline display requires a signed, installation-bound authorization lease of at most 24 hours, checked against monotonic elapsed time and wall time; rollback or boot/time discontinuity without rollback-resistant platform time fails closed until server contact. Caches purge on acknowledged deletion, retention-off deletion choice, logout, or account switch. Re-claim never exposes the prior account's retained history or deletes it implicitly. | Two-device/reinstall/re-claim convergence suite, cache/key/backup inspection, offline-lease expiry plus wall-clock rollback/reboot tests, version-conflict, and cursor/tombstone-retention audit |
| AP-09 | Privacy copy and verification distinguish Mochi-controlled storage from provider processing. | AP-10 | Account deletion requires re-authentication and explicit confirmation. Connected apps purge caches. A disconnected app may display its encrypted cache only until its signed authorization lease expires (at most 24 hours), then locks it and erases it on the next server contact returning an authoritative deletion/revocation or revoked-credential response; no remote-wipe claim is made before contact. Offline pagers are unauthorized at the gateway immediately and, on reconnect, purge binding/recovery state plus local Wi-Fi/custom cellular credentials before capture-gated setup. The UI distinguishes server deletion, connected-replica purge, offline lease/erasure timing, carrier-subscription action, and provider-retention boundaries. | Account-deletion fault/retry, offline-app lease/contact, offline-device/recovery/network-credential purge, carrier-deactivation prompt, app-cache/backup, export, service-storage, and provider-scope audit |

## Gates

- Gate A — interaction/acoustic mule: face simulator with sliding caption, gateway, and browser live-voice slice work; the purchased CoreS3 K128 demonstrates concurrent capture/playback, playback-cursor cancellation, caption trim on barge-in, and measurable AEC/barge-in feasibility; the initial external acoustic/privacy mule demonstrates an enclosed synchronized-reference path plus the capture-enable/indicator coupling on the bench gate rig. Push-to-talk-only operation is a documented target-gate failure, not a pass.
- Gate B — standalone cellular purchase: the Wi-Fi full-duplex live session passes its acoustic, privacy, and stability gates; target deployment region is confirmed; and modem/SIM compatibility is checked.
- Gate C — first custom PCB: exact compute, display/touch, synchronized microphone/capture and rendered-playback-reference path, AEC strategy, fail-closed capture-enable/indicator circuit, back-power-safe latching power path, amplifier/speaker, charger/gauge, connector set, USB host/device/debug roles, and one embedded modem transport are frozen from bench evidence; concurrent-load headroom, current peaks, battery budget, antenna keep-outs, and mechanical stack are measured. The companion-app track has fully passed (AP-01…AP-10, including frozen recovery limits/authority, claim-race tests, history convergence, account deletion, and provider-retention review), because per-device identity and setup/recovery material are manufacturing inputs and the history/privacy audits must precede any board that ships to testers.
- Gate D — integrated cellular: the external modem mule and intended embedded host pass attach, route/session transition, data, flow-control, reset/reconnect, power sequencing, peak/thermal, and antenna tests; a compliance lab has reviewed the plan.
- 5G gate — only opens if a measured product requirement cannot be met by LTE. “Newer” is not a requirement.
